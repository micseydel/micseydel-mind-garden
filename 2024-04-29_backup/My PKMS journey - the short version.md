# Historical

In high school, the code that I had written acted as a kind of narrow PKM, essentially just a reference for different patterns. When I eventually lost that code by accident, it hurt, and it would have hurt more had I realized what I was giving up by not being able to build on that code, missing out on years of compounding gains...

When I started working as a software engineer, the documentation and code at the company ended up being a kind of KMS that felt personal. Each time I left those companies, I left behind a lot of knowledge that I cannot get back easily. Besides the shared resources, I also kept personal-work notes at work for which I had no private equivalent, and I obviously lost those since they were owned by the company as well.

In 2020 I started taking notes more seriously. I had grown the habit at work but decided to put it toward my recently-started talk therapy. Each session was a note and I developed a template with before/during/after sessions, along with insights/action items in the after section. In 2021 I started a daily bullet journal and that allowed me to organically take more notes. Eventually I decided Google Docs was not the right tool and that I wanted a personal wiki instead.

# Today

After having learned about [Networked Thinking](https://www.appsntips.com/what-is-networked-thinking/) and making a detour through Roam Research I decided to use Obsidian. I wanted an [[Externalized cognition|external mind]] that could "think" for me and not just store things, but decided against Dataview for my person life-long basis.

At the time of this writing (not necessarily the same year as other notes in this mind garden), I'm [[My Tinker Cast]]. I refer to my note-making system as using atomic notes and MOCs, but the spirit is the same as the "networked thinking" link that originally inspired me to leave Google Docs for something shiny and new.
