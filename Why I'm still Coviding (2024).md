### "Just so you know"

> Covid is [airborne](https://www.bmj.com/content/378/bmj.o1929): it is in the exhaled breath of infected people. It is a highly contagious virus whose initial infection is typically followed by acute, delayed-onset, and long-term harms. [2/3 of covid spread](https://www.cdc.gov/coronavirus/2019-ncov/hcp/planning-scenarios.html#table-1) comes from [pre-symptomatic or asymptomatic people](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2774707) — they show no symptoms. It’s estimated that [30% of all covid cases](https://erictopol.substack.com/p/from-asymptomatic-covid-to-long-covid) are asymptomatic. Rapid tests [miss 90% of asymptomatic infections](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-02254-9). **Vaccination and treatment like Paxlovid are your** _**last**_ **lines of defense**. Covid-19 is not just a respiratory infection, [it is a multi-organ](https://insightplus.mja.com.au/2022/46/more-proof-covid-is-a-multi-system-cluster-bomb/), [systemic disease](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/covid-19/sars-cov-2-can-damage-mitochondrion-heart-other-organs-study-finds); a serious vascular, neurological, [immune-system-damaging](https://slate.com/technology/2023/01/immunity-covid-research-airborne-aids-debunk.html), sometimes [brain-damaging](https://www.health.harvard.edu/mind-and-mood/does-covid-19-damage-the-brain), ==randomly disabling== disease (CDC: [1 in 5](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/pressroom/nchs_press_releases/2022/20220622.htm); PHAC: [50%](https://www.ctvnews.ca/health/coronavirus/half-of-covid-19-patients-report-symptoms-after-12-weeks-says-new-phac-review-1.5500675)).
> 
> Covid-19 is a [biosafety level 3](https://www.criver.com/eureka/what-bsl-3-lab#:~:text=This%20level%20is%20the%20standard,required%20for%20potentially%20airborne%20diseases.) airborne virus that shows up with flu-like symptoms. Post-infection [immunity has shortened to 28 days](https://abc7news.com/ba5-covid-immunity-omicron-ba4-reinfections-how-long-are-you-immune-from-after-having-it/12047575/). Everyone infected is at high risk [for serious heart problems](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/2022/covid-and-the-heart-it-spares-no-one); the risk of deadly blood clots is [elevated for one year](https://www.express.co.uk/life-style/health/1672465/covid-coronavirus-blood-clots-risk). By infecting your cat or dog with covid, [you risk imparting them with brain damage](https://www.patreon.com/posts/we-need-to-talk-91185856) and delayed-onset, long-term harms similar to post-infection conditions in humans.
> 
> ==Long covid [can result from multiple reinfections](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/do-repeat-covid-infections-increase-the-risk-of-severe-disease-or-long-covid/), at [any severity of initial infection](https://www.fatherly.com/health/mild-covid-long-hauler-brain-heart-lungs) — [especially from just a second infection](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-42726-0?utm_source=CanadaHealthwatch).== Each reinfection does [cumulative](https://www.thedailybeast.com/how-covid-19-could-hit-you-harder-with-each-reinfection), [worsening damage](https://www.publichealthontario.ca/-/media/Documents/nCoV/voc/2022/07/evidence-brief-ba4-ba5-risk-assessment-jul-8.pdf?sc_lang=en) and [increases long covid risk](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/health/long-covid-warning-reinfection-b2111221.html). ==There is no treatment for long covid. There is no cure== for long covid. There is no prevention for long covid. **Anyone of any age or health status can get long covid.** [Here is what long covid looks like](https://skepchick.org/2023/03/physics-girl-the-devastating-effects-of-long-covid/). The [AMA wants people to know](https://www.ama-assn.org/delivering-care/public-health/what-doctors-wish-patients-knew-about-covid-19-reinfection) that getting reinfected is “akin to playing Russian Roulette.”
> 
> Immune system harm from mass covid infection and mass re-infection is now [tied to recent respiratory disease surges](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2814028?guestAccessKey=92cec39d-8e21-4bd0-9828-4bed1542cfb3&utm_source=silverchair&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=article_alert-jama&utm_content=olf&utm_term=011024&adv=null).
