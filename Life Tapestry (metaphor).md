If our lives are a tapestry
* Most people weave them by hand, often on autopilot or improv
* Some take a structured *loom* approach, which shows progress less **quickly at first and more quickly as time goes on**
* Practices like Building a Second Brain ([[Externalized cognition#The Extended Mind|thinking with apps]]) or digital Zettelkasten may even achieve a level of **power loom**
	* (something that can get work done without requiring a human to actively power it)
* [[My Tinker Cast|My Tinker Cast]] is like a [*programmable* power loom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacquard_machine)

[[My Tinker Cast|My Tinker Cast]] helps me weave and *[[Integration|integrate]]* the threads of my life. The focus on *tinkering* is an attempt to circumvent my brain's neuroses, inspiring curiosity instead of overwhelm.

The *programmable* and *power* parts are key as well. I can program my "loop" (my *cast*) to operate with an external power source, so that it has some degree of functionality regardless of my ability moment-to-moment.
