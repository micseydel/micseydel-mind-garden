- [Thanatotranscriptome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanatotranscriptome) per Wikipedia
    - > The **thanatotranscriptome** denotes all [RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribonucleic_acid "Ribonucleic acid") transcripts produced from the portions of the [genome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genome "Genome") still active or awakened in the internal organs of a body following its [death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death "Death"). It is relevant to the study of the [biochemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biochemistry "Biochemistry"), [microbiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microbiology "Microbiology"), and [biophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biophysics "Biophysics") of [thanatology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanatology "Thanatology"), in particular within [forensic science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forensic_science "Forensic science"). Some genes may continue to be expressed in [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_(biology) "Cell (biology)") for up to 48 hours after death, producing new [mRNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRNA "MRNA"). Certain genes that are generally inhibited since the end of fetal development may be expressed again at this time.
